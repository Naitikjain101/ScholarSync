# SIH 2026 Problem Statement 26238: Final Demo Script
**Unified Scholarship Mobile Application for Tribal Students**

## Preparation
- [ ] Run `npm run dev`
- [ ] Ensure `DEMO_MODE=true` is set.
- [ ] Open a fresh browser session (Incognito) resized to exactly **390px width** (mobile viewport).
- [ ] Navigate to `http://localhost:3000/`.

## The Demo Story
*“ScholarSync gives a tribal student one unified scholarship journey instead of forcing them through fragmented scholarship processes.”*

## The 4-Minute Flow

### 1. The Landing Page (0:00 - 0:30)
- **Say**: "This is ScholarSync, a unified mobile-first platform for all 5 MoTA scholarship schemes."
- **Scroll** through the landing page, showing the minimalist hero, the five schemes, and the three core intelligence concepts.
- **Action**: Click "Explore student experience" to begin.

### 2. Login & Student Identity (0:30 - 0:45)
- **Say**: "We are logging in as Kavita Meena, an ST student from Jharkhand."
- **Action**: Select the "Student" role. The system will log you into Kavita's unified dashboard.
- **Point out**: Her verified identity and ST status badge ("Demo Data").

### 3. Student Dashboard (0:45 - 1:15)
- **Say**: "Instead of 5 separate portals, Kavita sees her active Post-Matric application here, alongside other MoTA schemes."
- **Point out**: The "ALL SCHOLARSHIPS" section. Show how Top Class says "Conflict Detected" because ScholarSync prevents overlapping fellowship/scholarship benefits for the same student.
- **Action**: Click "Continue application".

### 4. Application 360 & Exceptions (1:15 - 2:00)
- **Say**: "Kavita's application is 'Under Verification'. However, there's a flag on her income certificate."
- **Action**: Click into the "Documents" tab.
- **Point out**: The "Required Documents" section ("Submit once. Reuse when eligible").
- **Action**: Open the Income Certificate.
- **Say**: "ScholarSync's document intelligence extracted an income of ₹3,00,000, but she declared ₹2,50,000. It doesn't blindly reject her — it routes the exception for manual review."

### 5. JAGO Companion (2:00 - 2:30)
- **Say**: "If Kavita is confused, she can ask JAGO."
- **Action**: Click the JAGO assistant icon.
- **Say**: "JAGO knows exactly who she is and why her application is stuck."
- **Action**: Click "Why is there a mismatch?" and wait for the response.

### 6. Ministry / Officer Operations (2:30 - 3:30)
- **Say**: "Now, let's see what happens on the backend."
- **Action**: Log out, then log in as "Ministry Official".
- **Point out**: The National Dashboard.
- **Action**: Scroll to the "Coverage Intelligence" section.
- **Say**: "ScholarSync proactively identifies tribal students who are enrolled in school but haven't received benefits. We found 3,538 potentially unreached students by cross-referencing UDISE+ and ST records."
- **Action**: Click into the "Student 360" view for a manual review case, demonstrating how human officers easily resolve exceptions.

### 7. Conclusion (3:30 - 4:00)
- **Say**: "From student discovery and automated verification to exception routing and Ministry intelligence, ScholarSync unifies the entire lifecycle."
- **Action**: End on the Ministry Dashboard showing the nationwide impact.
